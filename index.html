01 / HOW IT WORKS

From physical world to your screen.

Every student and evaluator can understand the 4 key stages of how data is created, packetized, and delivered.

01
Physical Sensor Measures heat, humidity, gas PPM, or soil moisture via analog voltage SOURCE
······›
02
Edge ESP32 Node 12-bit ADC converts voltage to numbers, adds timestamp & device ID EDGE
······›
03
Wireless RF Link Radio broadcasts a 24-byte packet through the air (2.4 GHz / LoRa) WIRELESS
······›
04
IoT Gateway Checks CRC checksum, saves to database, and plots live trend graph DEST

Virtual sensor lab.

Adjust environmental transducers or choose a real-world scenario. Watch how physical changes convert into digital ADC readings and travel across the RF link.

07 / DEPLOYMENT DOMAINS

Real-world applications.

Modular wireless sensor networks adapt seamlessly to diverse environmental, structural, and agricultural monitoring requirements.

01
[HVAC]

Smart Buildings & HVAC

Real-time indoor air quality (MQ-135) and temperature telemetry for energy-efficient climate control.

02
[AGRI]

Precision Agriculture

Distributed capacitive soil moisture and ambient humidity monitoring for automated irrigation scheduling.

03
[STRUCT]

Structural Health

Continuous vibration, strain, and environmental telemetry for bridge, tunnel, and dam safety inspections.

[CAMPUS]

Academic & Research Labs

A hands-on testbed for studying embedded IoT architectures, wireless protocols, and telemetry pipelines.
